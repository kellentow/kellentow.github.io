**Discord Bot Privacy Policy**

# Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Command Data**: Content of messages starting with `!` (commands only).
- **Economy Data**: Virtual currency balances, transaction history generated by commands.
- **User Metadata**: Discord username, guild (Discord server) ID and name, message timestamps.

## 2. How We Collect Information
Information is collected automatically when you interact with the Bot (e.g., issuing commands).

## 3. Purpose of Data Collection
- **Operation**: To process commands and maintain the economy system.
- **Maintenance**: To debug, monitor performance, and improve Bot features.

## 4. Data Storage & Security
- Data is stored on private servers with restricted access.
- We implement reasonable technical measures (e.g., encryption at rest, access controls) to protect data.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do **not** share your data with third parties, except as required by law with a warrant or to comply with Discord’s policies.

## 6. Data Retention
We retain data as long as necessary to provide the Bot’s services or to comply with legal obligations. Users may request deletion of their data by contacting us.

## 7. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

- Access the data we hold about you.
- Request correction or deletion of your data.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.

To exercise any rights, contact us at kellencoding@icloud.com.

## 8. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify users via a message in Discord or by updating the link. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 9 Deletion
A **User** may request deletion of all identifying information (User IDs, Guild IDs) by emailing kellencoding@icloud.com. Please include the relevant Guild ID(s) and User ID. We will verify that you are the owner of the specified Discord account or guild before processing. Requests will be processed within 30 days of receipt.
